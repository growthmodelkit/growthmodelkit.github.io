Result

Growth. The growth multiplier: all users that one new user turns into by month 12, itself included. The users it invites invite others too, so growth comes in waves.

Growth multiplier by month 12×1.65

Limit. With a k-factor below 1, every next wave is smaller, and growth stops at a limit. With 1 or more, it goes on until the market runs out.
