Levers

Users can move one step up and pay more. Under each lever, set how much you could grow the group to see the effect. Each group keeps its own revenue per user.

Core users (from casual). Casual users become core when the product finds a place in their week: a regular task, a reason to come back. The core group grows by taking users from the casual group.
