The whole picture

Five tools, one model. Activation, Engagement and Virality each look at one part of the product and give their numbers to MAU and LTV, which turn them into users and money. Retention is king: MAU and LTV both stand on the retention curve.

How to use them

Start with MAU. Put in your active users, new users per month, retention and revenue per user, and see where MAU and revenue go. LTV takes the same numbers and shows what one new user is worth.

Find the lever. The “Levers” section of MAU shows which number moves revenue the most, and the chart shows all levers together.

See what it takes. Activation, Engagement and Virality each show what has to change in the product to move one lever. Their “Use in model” section says which numbers to put back into MAU.

Check a channel. A channel pays off when LTV is above the cost of a user. A viral loop lowers this cost: each new user also brings others.
