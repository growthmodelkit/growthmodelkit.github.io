Activation. The share of new users who reach the habit moment: they come back and use the product again and again. Pick an action that shows the habit, such as three visits in the first week, and take the share of new users who do it.

Retention. The share of a cohort still active when the retention curve flattens, for each group on its own: users who activate and users who don't. Split your retention curve by the habit action to get both numbers. Users who activate are usually much more likely to stay.
