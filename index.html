Simple calculators to support decisions with numbers

What it is

Growth Model Kit is napkin math for product growth. Put in a few numbers, see how users and revenue move over time, and find the levers that matter.

Tools

  • MAU — Where will your users and revenue go from here?
  • LTV — What is one new user worth?
  • Activation — How does better onboarding lift retention?
  • Engagement — How does engagement lift revenue per user?
  • Virality — How many users does a new one bring?
